# RINGSIDE Roadmap

## Completed — V0.4

- Persistent fictional roster, 0–25 attributes, Current Ability/Potential/Market Value, contracts and finance.
- Local browser UI plus text CLI and FastAPI backend.
- Men's and women's divisions, with separate storylines and mandatory representation on each full show.
- 100–120-minute show booking gate and two-slot per storyline cap.
- Match psychology/stipulations, fatigue costs, recovery, promo brawls and staged match highlights.
- Hiring creative heads, contextual recommendations, creative promises linked to actionable Inbox tasks.
- Media assignments with consent chance, owner/wrestler interactions, personality and locker-room disagreements.
- V0.3 JSON save migration and V0.4 acceptance suite.

## Recommended V0.5 — Deeper world simulation

- Rival companies autonomously book shows, negotiate for talent and lose/gain viewership.
- Distinct wrestler demands, contract counters and lasting commitments, more nuanced backstage relationships.
- Multi-week storyline scheduling and creative staff contracts/poaching.
- Move sets tailored to individual wrestler profiles (rather than style pools), layered stamina effects and match timing.
- Optional auto-fill suggestions for the final few minutes of a card (but never auto-book without player approval).

## Recommended V0.6 — Career & production

- Champions, titles, rankings, tag teams and factions.
- Talent schools, training, injury/recovery safeguards and wrestler retirement/reinvention.
- TV deals, sponsorship negotiation, merchandise, media partnerships and regional market awareness.
- Explicit game settings for show duration and division policies.

## Later

- Optional narrative-text generation using a local LLM, with the numerical simulation remaining authoritative.
- More advanced fan demographics, camera production, social media and press reaction.
- Campaign selection, cloud-compatible saves, more frontend polish and accessibility work.

## Version 0.5 — implemented
- Separate wrestler-management and contract negotiation rooms with dialogue options.
- Persistent scouting, coach training, NPC company simulation and world headlines.
- Creative room promises prepared directly in Show Booker and visible on wrestler profiles.
- Calendar, 13 annual PPVs and metric history.
- V0.4 save migration.

## Next phases
- Schedule customization, PPV planning with multi-week commitments and multiple brands.
- Deep NPC talent bidding, network rights and competing creative strategies.
- Trainer compatibility, medical availability and long-term aging balance.
- Better narrative content variety, user authored characters and storyline editing.
- Optional **controlled local Ollama** narrative generation, using typed JSON facts and engine-validated outputs; keep simulation fully deterministic without an LLM.
